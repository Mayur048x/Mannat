Project Overview

A Landmark of
Thoughtful Proportions.

Directly fronting a wide 30-meter D.P. Road immediately next to the APMC Market in prime Kalyan West, Seasons Mannat balances tranquil sanctuary living with unmatched urban access. Engineered with low-density twin-tower layouts, it redefines modern high-rise living through generous proportions and holistic wellness.

  • Curated Residences: Thoughtfully planned 2 BHK Luxury, 3 BHK Royale, and palatial 4 BHK Crown floor layouts with optimal ventilation and zero dead space.
  • Continuous 4-Foot Balcony Decks: Every single 2, 3, and 4 BHK home extends onto an expansive running open deck off the living and dining spaces.
  • Rare Low Density: Only 5 residences per floor plate, served by 4 high-speed elevators (including a dedicated fire-safety evacuation lift).
  • Grand Arrival Experience: Double-height covered vehicular drop-off circle with designer air-conditioned entrance lobbies and smooth dual-podium ramps.
  • High-Street Retail Promenade: Ground-level commercial boutiques fronting the 30M road, bringing daily conveniences right to your doorstep.
  • Municipal & Safety Sanctions: Backed by KDMC municipal approvals, CFO fire safety clearance, and registered under MahaRERA No. PM1331012601752.
